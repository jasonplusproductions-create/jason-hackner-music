About Jason Hackner

Jason Hackner Music brings classic soul and pop to life in Charlotte — Motown warmth, R&B groove, and the kind of live energy that fills a room with light.

From intimate venues to festival stages, Jason leads hopeful, high-spirit performances shaped by exceptional musicianship and a love for songs that move people.

Often joined by: Scott McLaughlin, Guillermo Villar, Parker Foley, Faithful Gwenhah, Tom Hanchett, Paul Walker, Tony Arreaza, Shana Blake, Jenny Brooks, Tony Torres, and Boo English — with gratitude to John Tosco.

Formerly Jason Plus the MLC. Past collaborators: Mark Larson.
